# 31 - Synthetic power results

Generated by `scripts/power_sim.py` (500 simulations per cell, 199 permutations each, alpha 0.05, one-sided). Data from `src/dund/synth.py`: base rate 5%, flag rate 2%, about 4 ticker-days per ticker, log-normal ticker multiplier (sd 0.5) so outcomes correlate within ticker. Test: flagged hit rate vs unflagged, flags permuted within ticker (cluster-respecting). Pure stdlib; numpy/scipy are not in the venv.

## Empirical rejection rate by number of ticker-days N

| scenario | N=2000 | N=5000 | N=10000 | N=20000 | N=40000 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| null 5%->5% (false positives) | 0.024 | 0.036 | 0.030 | 0.040 | 0.028 |
| 5%->7% | 0.056 | 0.140 | 0.222 | 0.368 | 0.612 |
| 5%->10% | 0.180 | 0.426 | 0.708 | 0.968 | 0.994 |
| 5%->15% | 0.462 | 0.870 | 0.992 | 1.000 | 1.000 |

## N (ticker-days) needed for 80% power

| lift | N for 80% power |
|---|---|
| 5% -> 15% | about 4,500 |
| 5% -> 10% | about 13,500 |
| 5% -> 7% | more than 40,000 (extrapolated roughly 55,000 to 65,000) |

## Reading

The test is slightly conservative (false positives 2.4 to 4.0%, below the nominal 5%), so there is no inflation from clustering. Power is driven by the number of flagged items, only about 2% of N, so 13,500 ticker-days means roughly 270 flags. A large lift (5% to 15%) is detectable with a few thousand ticker-days, which one to two months of the 14 subs would cover per doc `28`. A moderate lift (5% to 10%) needs about a year of data, and a small one (5% to 7%) is out of reach at this flag rate without raising the flag rate or pooling sources. Caveats: the flag rate is fixed at 2%, ticker clustering is mild, and a regime shift (supported in the generator, `regime_shift_day`) would shrink the effective lift further.
